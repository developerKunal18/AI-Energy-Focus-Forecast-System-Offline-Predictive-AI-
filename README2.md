# 🔮 AI Energy & Focus Forecast System 

## 💡 Overview
The **AI Energy & Focus Forecast System** is an offline Python application that predicts **tomorrow’s productivity level** based on today’s energy, focus, stress, and work hours.

It simulates how **personal analytics, coaching AI, and performance forecasting systems** help individuals plan smarter and avoid burnout.

---

## 🚀 Features

### ⚡ Energy & Focus Analysis
- Takes daily inputs:
  - Energy level (1–5)
  - Focus level (1–5)
  - Stress level (1–5)
  - Work hours

### 🔮 Productivity Forecast
- Predicts tomorrow’s productivity as:
  - High
  - Moderate
  - Low

### 🧭 AI Recommendations
- Provides actionable advice to:
  - Reduce stress
  - Improve focus
  - Balance workload
  - Plan tasks effectively

### 🌐 Fully Offline
- No APIs
- No internet required
- Runs locally in terminal
